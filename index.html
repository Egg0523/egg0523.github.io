Projects

Dual-System Vision-and-Language Navigation for a Humanoid

AgiBot · Motion Intelligence Group · 2026 · DualVLN reproduction, self-collected data · Simulation
  • Built a VLN data pipeline from scratch: collected 4,229 R2R/RxR trajectories across 60 Matterport3D scenes, projected 3-D trajectories onto 2-D egocentric views with depth-based visibility filtering to discard occluded points, and segmented them into 251,916 training samples spanning pixel-goal grounding, self-directed view adjustment, and STOP supervision.
  • LoRA-finetuned System 2 (Qwen-VL-2.5-7B, where the paper fully finetunes) and trained the matching flow-matching diffusion-transformer System 1, conditioned jointly on latent goal queries and high-frequency RGB, emitting 32 dense waypoints.
  • VLN-CE RxR val-unseen: SR 59.3 / SPL 51.6, level with the 59.6 / 51.8 obtained by re-running the official weights in the same evaluation setup — at substantially lower training data and GPU memory cost.

Perceptive Forward-Dynamics Navigation on the X2 Humanoid

AgiBot · Motion Intelligence Group · 2026 · FDM (RSS 2025) migration and extension · Simulation and hardware
  • Migrated a learned perceptive Forward Dynamics Model to the X2 humanoid: proprioceptive history plus an elevation map predict 5 s of future SE(2) poses and failure risk, feeding an MPPI sampling planner that emits velocity commands directly — removing hand-tuned cost maps entirely.
  • Deployed the full perception–planning–control pipeline on hardware. The robot traverses doorways, ascending and descending stairs, pillar fields, and corridors, reaching 4 m goals within 0.3 m of position error.
  • Extended the method to moving obstacles, a case the original paper explicitly lists as untested: 80% avoidance success against a 1 m/s moving obstacle under a 10 Hz perception–replanning loop, with the remaining 20% stopping and waiting rather than colliding — no collisions across the runs.
  • Substituted FDM for System 1 as the action expert, projecting System 2's pixel goals into body-frame waypoints — the path that carries language-driven navigation onto the real robot.

Attention-Based Perceptive Locomotion for a Humanoid

AgiBot · Motion Intelligence Group · 2026 · AME-2 reproduction, Isaac Lab · Simulation
  • Reproduced attention-based map encoding — local CNN features weighted by multi-head attention against a query built from global pooled features and proprioception, concentrating the policy on critical foothold regions — together with uncertainty-aware neural elevation mapping and teacher–student distillation (PPO, action distillation, and representation alignment).
  • Transferred from the paper's quadruped and point-foot biped platforms to the armed X2 humanoid, which carries a higher center of mass and a smaller support polygon: 90% teacher / 75% student success on unseen terrain, against 95.2% / 82.4% for the paper's quadruped — a distillation gap in line with the original.
  • Diagnosed and fixed foothold drift during consecutive stair ascents, traced to odometry–elevation-map misalignment, along with gait instability on descent.
