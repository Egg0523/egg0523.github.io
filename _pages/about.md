I am a master's student in Mechanical Engineering and Applied Mechanics (Mechatronics & Robotic Systems) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.me.upenn.edu/), where I work on **humanoid robot locomotion and navigation**. I am currently an intern on the Motion Intelligence team at [AgiBot (智元机器人)](https://www.zhiyuan-robot.com/) X-Lab in Shenzhen, working on reinforcement-learning-based locomotion, sim-to-real transfer, and long-horizon navigation for humanoid robots.

Before Penn, I received my B.E. from the ZJU-UIUC Institute, where I was a core member of the ZJUI Meta RoboMaster team (2023 National 3V3 Second Prize).

Research interests
======

* **Legged locomotion** — reinforcement learning for humanoid and quadruped robots (Isaac Lab/Sim, Legged Gym), terrain-aware policies, sim-to-real deployment
* **Navigation** — vision-language navigation (VLN) and long-horizon planning for mobile robots
* **Mechatronics & hardware** — motor control, embedded systems (ESP32 / AVR), and hands-on prototyping (3D printing, laser cutting, circuit assembly)

Selected experience
======

* **AgiBot X-Lab, Motion Intelligence team** (2026 – present) — humanoid locomotion and navigation: RL training in simulation, sim-to-real deployment, and VLN-based navigation
* **Quadruped loco-manipulation** — PPO-based whole-body control on a Unitree GO2 with a mounted arm
* **Tendon-driven dexterous hand** — design and control of an underactuated robotic hand
* **UAV SE(3) path planning** — trajectory planning for aerial robots
* **ZJUI Meta RoboMaster team** — semi-autonomous combat robot; 2023 national 3V3 Second Prize
